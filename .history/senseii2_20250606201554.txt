Senseii Search
Senseii accounts for the economic weight backlinked to a user’s IP, and searches for relevant
content according to its economic weight.
If peers’ IP were only searched for by cost to advertise, ads from big corporations would always
rise to the top of Senseii’s search index and drown out small entrepreneurs and startups, just
like they do in Google searches today.
Instead, Senseii judges users according to the economic weight attached to their IP and the IP
of their peers. For example, IP associated with a well-known HandCash handle, with links to
many other HandCash users, will climb Senseii’s ranking faster than one with payments and
links to fewer.
Senseii seamlessly pays more attention to content with valuable associated IP and stronger
social bonds than to bots and trolls without any economic weight (a.k.a “Skeletons”).
Using this framework, users can easily query other users directly and ask to see their IP on any
given topic in a secure environment, to assess if they’re the right match for a job, contract,
position or other designation.

Senseii Social Graph Module:
The Senseii Social Graph is built on a recursive, economically weighted graph architecture
called ‘MetaGraph’ where streaming payments
reward the Original Poster (OP), or drain them,
according to how many interactions they gather,
and what contractual obligations have been set.
Skins built on top of the MetaGraph can look like
Twitter, or Facebook, but comments have
micropayments as small $0.0001 attached, so
popular tweets can grow in value as they attract
attention, and can be culled (sold) by the user as
and when they wish to recapture the fruits of their
intellectual labour.
Additionally, the value of the Bitcoin attached to
any given comment will likely fluctuate upwards
the longer users refuse to sell ownership of their
ideas. This is akin to ‘HODLing’ their intellectual
capital on the MetaNet until they feel it is valuable enough to sell. Senseii LTD anticipates that
this form of HODLing (and economic cultivation of a users IP by its user) will in time completely
replace the archaic HODL mentality of the BTC culture.
Self-Organising Intellectual Capital:
The Senseii Social Graph (or MetaGraph) is a self-organizing, self-referential model, that
actively rewards ‘Proof of Work’ (proof of participation) by design.
In other words, the more work a user puts into building connections in their social graph by
adding content and interacting with other users, the more trusted peer reviews they accrue and
the more money they make.
As a result of these interactions, we expect them to offer more contracts, which will lead to the
faster incorporation of bodies of valuable knowledge around them and the creation and
production of more valuable companies, with more verified IP and more potential to raise
capital.
Linking Pseudonymous Identities:
Pseudonymous profiles (nyms) accrue value in the form of Social Proof, according to how
reliable they are, how accurate the content they share is, and how many links to related
valuable IP they have in their network.
A nym’s network is its net worth.
Senseii is designed explicitly to foster a community of student entrepreneurs who can share
their ideas freely, and not fear losing control of their own intellectual property. Good quality IP
can be quickly and efficiently capitalised, and teachers promoted appropriately according to their
knowledge and history. The emphasis is always on learning and building, and the proper
assignment of intellectual property. Trolls are naturally excluded in the same way disruptive
students are excluded from classrooms.
Senseii Wallet Module
Senseii Wallet Module teaches students how to keep track of streaming revenues and multiple
balances for the different tokenized IP they own and lets them arrange simultaneous payments
to an unbounded number of stakeholders.
Senseii allows students to share balances privately and publicly across organisations and
groups, giving them the freedom to start liquidity pools, lending circles, credit agreements with
repayment and interest payments, and annuities and bonus payouts upon the completion of
contractual arrangements.
Senseii Ledger Module
An extension of the Senseii Wallet, ‘Senseii Ledger’ allows for the real-time tracking of balances
and content in a spreadsheet format, such that inventory can be tracked and revenue accounted
for in a single spreadsheet.
Senseii Store Module (The Token Library)
Senseii Store allows users to index and reference their IP according to their content type like
Video, Audio or Picture, or by volume (quantity and rarity of their tokens), or by their economic
weight etc. Once added to their store, a user’s IP can be used in the composition of smart
contracts using Kitana for quick and easy deployment.
Senseii Signature Module
A core part of ‘Mimoto’, the Senseii Signature Module, ensures users can securely sign their
contracts by employing HMACs with combined cryptographic algorithms (e.g. Sha-1 &
RIPEMD), to generate HD keys that link multiple single-use signatures back to their real identity
securely, without compromising their privacy.
Mimoto allows users to prove to the tax-office or any other legal entity that requires compliance
that they created, bought, rented or sold, any token for which there was a capital gains event, or
other reporting need.
The terms and conditions of use (the EULA) of Senseii on any device explicitly states that
without first linking a set of keys to a legal identity, a user cannot use Senseii to lay legal claim
to the ownership of any Tokenised IP generated whilst using Senseii on their device. In other
words, unless a user takes the time to register their identity on our product properly, Senseii
does not guarantee users will be able to claim ownership of their IP. However: it is anticipated
that for most mobile phone users, the use of Senseii on their device will implicitly link them to
their government identity via their phone contract, and that in most cases, this will be accepted
in a court of law as valid evidence of their identity in the case of any disputes.
Senseii Contract Market
Contract Market allows users to further tokenise and sell the contracts they have composed
such that other users can buy them and incorporate them into their own businesses and
business logic easily and simply. Composable contracts may even be melted down and
re-constituted, provided that more than 51% of the stakeholders in any given token assent to
their melting and recomposition.

Senseii NFT Camera Module
A quick and easy way to introduce students to the idea of tokenizing data and capturing their
own IP is through the ‘Senseii Camera’ module. Senseii NFT Camera allows users to convert
their phone camera pictures into NFTs instantly, and publish them to the blockchain as
encrypted files to which only the user has a key.
Users can then generate multiple keys to the same picture, and sell these different keys to
different users to decrypt the pictures they buy. This methodology ensures that a user who buys
a picture and then sells their key can always be identified. (This is useful if a buyer decides to
break a contract with a seller and publishes a key that they’ve bought on a file-sharing network).
In this way, consumers are incentivised to remain honest since their signature will match with
the key they’ve bought.
An unlimited quantity of keys can be listed and sold using the Senseii exchange module, and
revenues from sales can be streamed to multiple recipients (for example, model, studio, makeup
artist, etc.).
Using the above methodology, companies like ‘Ninja Punk Girls LTD’ can expand massively to
include thousands of content producers and millions of customers, and content for distributed
CDNs can be split up amongst millions of peers, ready for delivery to consumers.
Senseii Image Exchange Module
Users can list, licence, sell and rent out their tokenized pictures using the Senseii Exchange
Module, which in turn allows users to create multiple streams of revenue by selling selective
access to many pictures and picture collections.
Collections can be previewed by offering one picture for free, and subsequent pictures for a
price. Unique cat photos for example could be sold for as little as $0.0001 each, and a user
could build an entire multi-million dollar business by selling and licensing only cat photos.
Senseii Stream Module
Senseii Stream allows the tokenization of users' videos such that a single video can be broken
down into millions of separate and individual frames and tokenized in Senseii Store.
These tokens can be encrypted using HMACs (as above), such that access can be sold
selectively to small individual scenes in a movie or the whole movie, or indeed single frames.
This methodology also allows large files to be distributed across the network similar to Bittorrent
files and re-assembled by users, who buy unique keys (as above) inside magnet links on the
Senseii Exchange.
We anticipate this kind of distributed streaming methodology to replace CDNs altogether in
future, as it creates a very robust data-delivery network where payment for hosting can be
spread widely amongst the broadest possible network of peers, and making Denial-of-Service
attacks unfeasibly costly.

Senseii Teams
Having posted a personal profile, users can select other member’s profiles and include them in
their organisations. Senseii Teams allows users to place team members in hierarchical
flowcharts and add payrolls, streaming payouts, dividends and share options, and even set KPIs
for their employees and add bonuses for sales targets etc.
Senseii Teams Module also allows for the incorporation of a team into a single company token,
with all its contractually binding logic included, such that shares in the company can be issued
and sold on the Senseii exchange or any other supported exchanges. These incorporated
tokens may have a single issuer, and a single price, but can represent the interests of millions or
even billions of potential stakeholders in the enterprise.
Using this architecture allows for the creation, cultivation and trade of any size organisation
without disrupting the organisation's suppliers, customers, shareholders, staff etc. and without
the need to rely on archaic legacy bureaucracies to rubber stamp the sale and transfer ahead of
the event.
All business activities done on the Senseii platform can be proved backwards in time, provided
users adhere to the Senseii app’s EULA.
Senseii Share Module
A crucial aspect of the Senseii platform is transparency. Users can opt to share any aspect of
their business with any other user for any reason using Senseii Share. For example, an
entrepreneur might choose to share their company’s revenues with their shareholders in real
time, or their marketing department's expenses with their data-analytics department.
The advantage of Senseii Share is that any part of the business can be made transparent to any
other part, or even made public, which in turn can help it to optimise, be accountable and
transparent to its shareholders and the general public.
This ability to share information publicly in a secure, transparent and auditable way will lend
massive amounts of confidence and value to collective enterprises.
Because of the availability of such a system, banks and institutions who rely on the approval of
the electorate and the continued flow of public money will increasingly want to demonstrate that
they can prove their reserves, and it will lend credence and weight to their legitimacy by
demonstrating that they can be trusted.
Integration with a public global monetary system, rather than a collection of private monetary
systems, will add strength and a new level of dynamism to companies who choose to build on
the blockchain natively.
Additionally, Senseii Share will allow users to issue shares in any part of their business,
enabling them to raise additional capital to fund just those parts of the business which need it.
Senseii Wrap
While smart-contracting can already be done with native BSV, increasingly stable-coins, other
cryptocurrencies and CBDCs will need to be incorporated into user’s smart contracts.
To achieve this, they will need to be locked into escrow contracts such that they can be
unlocked according to the terms of the smart contracts they are in. Senseii Wrap allows the
wrapping of these alternative digital assets so that they can be easily listed and added to
Senseii Smart Contracts.
Senseii takes advantage of the fact that packets over IPv6 can themselves be tokenized as they
cross the network. This means that packets which contain cryptocurrency tokens or digital
currency (CBDC) tokens can be locked and unlocked in smart contracts on the BSV network.
Because of BSV’s natural capacity to scale to accommodate all traffic over IPv4 and IPv6,
Senseii can offer its users the ability to capture and contract with so-called ‘Crypto Currency
Networks’’ on all other public blockchains, and by offering this feature, Senseii can help onboard
the $1 Trillion Dollar Crypto-currency industry onto BSV, by cutting their cost of settlement down
by 99%.
